<strong>Fig. S29.</strong> Frequency of cooperators and the difference in that frequency between the two populations under asymmetric cooperation costs and asymmetric information costs (c₀ = 0.1, c₁ = 0.2) at group size 4. (<strong>a–e</strong>) Population 0 (lower c). (<strong>f–j</strong>) Population 1 (higher c). (<strong>k–o</strong>) Difference in frequency of cooperators (population 0 − population 1). (<strong>a, f, k</strong>) When no cooperation mechanisms are allowed. (<strong>b, g, l</strong>) When short-memory partner choice (P) is allowed. (<strong>c, h, m</strong>) When direct reciprocity (M) is allowed. (<strong>d, i, n</strong>) When partner choice and direct and indirect reciprocity (IMP) are allowed. (<strong>e, j, o</strong>) When partner choice and direct and indirect reciprocity, including long-memory variants, (IJMPQ) are allowed.
